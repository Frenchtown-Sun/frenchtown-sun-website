**SUNDAY, SEPTEMBER 6**
* **RiverFest, 11 a.m. – 7 p.m.,** Bridge St. & Sunbeam Lenape Park  
  *A street-fair celebration of life on the river featuring two main stages for live music and performances, 50+ art vendors, food trucks and local food vendors, Wild Fern beer tent, Frenchtown Vikings and more\! Follow the Riverfest Facebook page for updates or visit lovefrenchtown.org. Bridge Street will be closed for the duration of the event to host local vendors and provide a safe experience for visitors. A bus shuttle will be transporting visitors from Frenchtown Field to the downtown throughout the day. Visitors are encouraged to take advantage of the parking at Frenchtown Field and at Kathee's General Store*.
