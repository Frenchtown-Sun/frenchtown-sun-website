Delaware Valley Regional High School 's journalism program instituted the first ever “Rick Epstein Excellence in Scholastic Journalism Award” and gave the first accolades to Gianna Roberts, Abigail Eckert and Juliana Findeis of the Class of 2026\. Rick was present at the June Senior Awards Ceremony and was invited to the stage to award the students with journalism program advisor Brian Smith.
