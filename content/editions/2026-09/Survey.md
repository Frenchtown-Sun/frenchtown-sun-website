We are pleased to celebrate the sixth issue of _The Frenchtown Sun_ this month. Since April, _The Sun_ has published with the support of the Borough Council and funding from the borough’s Age-Friendly grant through the New Jersey Department of Human Services. Mailed to every borough resident—with additional copies placed at key locations throughout town—this initial six-month run served as a pilot effort to gauge local interest in a dedicated news and features publication.

The response to _The Sun_ has been overwhelmingly positive. As our initial grant funding for this pilot effort concludes, _The Sun_ has received a generous grant from the Fargo Family Foundation, allowing us to continue publishing over the coming months (read more about the Fargo Family Foundation [here](/editions/2026-09/history_spotlight_the_fargo_family_foundation)). We are tremendously grateful for the support of the Fargo Family Foundation to enable continued publication of _The Sun_ for several forthcoming months.

During this time, our editorial team will collaborate with the borough to determine next steps for _The Sun_. 
To ensure our future model meets the needs of Frenchtown’s residents, businesses and community leaders, we need to hear from you. Please take a few moments to complete our brief anonymous survey to help us better understand your interests and priorities. You can find the survey at [frenchtownsun.net/survey](/survey).

**We would appreciate your feedback by September 30.**
