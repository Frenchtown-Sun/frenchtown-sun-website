_The Frenchtown Sun_ is published monthly by the 
Borough of Frenchtown to provide its residents 
with local news and community updates. 

The content of _The Sun_ is guided by the editorial 
team of Ted Brandston, Randi Eckel, Rick 
Epstein, John Gattuso, Jeanne Herb, Amelia 
Rodriguez and Maria Whelan. John Dougherty 
serves as the Borough Council liaison.

_The Sun_ is delivered monthly free of charge to all 
Frenchtown Borough residents. An online edition 
is available at [frenchtownsun.net](http://frenchtownsun.net). 

Borough residents can request postal delivery or 
contact the editorial team at 
newsletter@frenchtownboro.com or mail _The Frenchtown Sun_, 29 Second Street, Frenchtown, 
N.J. 08825.
