September 20-26 is Falls Prevention Awareness Week. Falls can be prevented by removing home hazards, practicing balance exercises and regularly checking medications and vision. More information regarding Falls Prevention can be found at the Council on Aging website: [www.ncoa.org/older-adults/health/prevention/falls-prevention/](https://www.ncoa.org/older-adults/health/prevention/falls-prevention/).

In addition to its regularly scheduled fitness and social recreational programs and classes, the Hunterdon County Senior Center has several interesting specials in September: Abe and Mary Todd Lincoln in conversation, a chamber music event transporting the audience back to the 18th century and more\!

Italian and Spanish language classes will resume after a summer break. For these and other programs, visit the Center’s website: [www.co.hunterdon.nj.us/495/Senior-Center](https://www.co.hunterdon.nj.us/495/Senior-Center) or call the center at <a href="tel:908-788-1359">908-788-1359</a>.
