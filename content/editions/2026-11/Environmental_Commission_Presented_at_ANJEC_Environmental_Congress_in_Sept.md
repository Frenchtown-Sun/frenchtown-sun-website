As previously reported by *The Sun,* the Frenchtown Environmental Commission (FEC) received an Association of NJ Environmental Commissions (ANJEC) Open Space Stewardship grant in May of 2026 for the weevil project.

Commission member Armin Zomorodi shared a slideshow presentation at the 53rd ANJEC Environmental Congress on September 25 as part of a panel of other Open Space Stewardship Grantees.

Zomorodi participates in the FEC in hopes of improving access and stewardship of Frenchtown’s green spaces and the sustainability and affordability of the town infrastructure and policies.
