Join Mayor Brad Myhre and Borough Council members on Saturday, November 7 from 10:00 a.m. – 12:00 p.m. at Frenchtown Elementary School to discuss efforts underway in the borough to promote an Age-Friendly Frenchtown. The first hour of the Town Hall will focus on conversation with Mayor Myhre and Council members; the second hour will be an “open house” with a variety of government agencies and nonprofit organizations that provide services to older residents. This is a valuable opportunity for Frenchtown’s older residents to learn about programs and services available to them locally. In June 2026, Borough Council adopted an Age-Friendly Action Plan that was the result of a year-long planning process supported by a grant from the New Jersey Department of Human Services: [frenchtownboro.com/community-services/age-friendly-frenchtown/](https://frenchtownboro.com/community-services/age-friendly-frenchtown/).

Coffee and snacks will be available. Borough residents who need accommodations or assistance to attend the Town Hall, please reach out to the Borough Clerk at <a href="tel:908-996-4524">908-996-4524</a> or [clerk@frenchtownboro.gov](mailto:clerk@frenchtownboro.gov).
