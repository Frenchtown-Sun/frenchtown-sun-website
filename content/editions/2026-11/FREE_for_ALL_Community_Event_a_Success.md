The River Bridges Community Group expresses their sincere thanks to all who participated in October’s community-wide FREE for ALL event.

Over 30 area households shared items that they no longer needed with their neighbors—many items were passed along to appreciative new homes and saved from being discarded into landfills.

River Bridges is looking forward to making FREE for ALL an annual event and hoping to see more Frenchtownians next year!
