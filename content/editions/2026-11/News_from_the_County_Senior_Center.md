In November, the Senior Center will celebrate Friendsgiving with a pie and ice cream social—for more information, visit: [co.hunterdon.nj.us/495/Senior-Center#docaccess-ba438385277df5f5047a1a103143e670](https://www.co.hunterdon.nj.us/495/Senior-Center).

In addition to the Center’s regularly scheduled fitness and social recreational programs and classes, specials in November include a sound healing workshop, a talk by Larry Kidder on George Washington’s historic crossing and workshops on Senior Safety.

Pre-registration is required for all special events. For these and other November programs at the Senior Center, visit the Center’s website at [co.hunterdon.nj.us/495/Senior-Center](https://www.co.hunterdon.nj.us/495/Senior-Center) or call <a href="tel:908-788-1359">908-788-1359</a>.

Membership to the Senior Center is free; you must be a resident of Hunterdon County and 60 years of age.
