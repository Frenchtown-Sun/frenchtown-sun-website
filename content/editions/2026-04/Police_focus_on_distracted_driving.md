The Police Department received various grants 
throughout the year for traffic enforcement. April is 
Distracted Driving Awareness Month. Officers will 
be conducting distracted driving enforcement 
details throughout the borough. 

The U.S. Drug Enforcement Agency (DEA) 
National Prescription Drug Takeback Day is 
Saturday, April 25, from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. This 
program offers safe, free and anonymous disposal of 
unused or expired medications. The Frenchtown 
Police Department is a collection site year-round.
