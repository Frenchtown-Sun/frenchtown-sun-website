The Planning/Zoning Board of Adjustment meets 
at 7 p.m. on the fourth Wednesday of every month at 
Borough Hall. All meetings are also streamed live, 
via Zoom on the borough webpage, and past 
meetings can be viewed on the borough’s YouTube 
channel.
