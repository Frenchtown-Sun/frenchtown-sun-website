Earlier this spring, the borough launched a new community-updates system, powered by Constant Contact, enabling residents to keep up with what’s happening in our town. Those who sign up will receive updates on local news, events and important announcements. To sign up, visit: [https://lp.constantcontactpages.com/sl/zNyK2m8](https://lp.constantcontactpages.com/sl/zNyK2m8) or visit Borough Hall for a sign-up sheet.
