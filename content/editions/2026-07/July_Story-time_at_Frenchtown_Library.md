On Tuesday mornings in July, Miss Kelly and her favorite canine Marley will host children aged 2-5 years for story-time at the library. Readings will be from 11-11:30 a.m.

On Thursday mornings, the library will host Melissa Nemitz from Hunterdon County Library for story-time at 11:30 am. These readings are appropriate for all ages.
