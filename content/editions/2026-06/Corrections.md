* *The Sun*’s May issue invited residents to reserve Borough Park for personal use. Residents can only reserve the park’s picnic pavilion.
* Also in May, *The Sun* misstated the amount of the grant from the state Department of Transportation to improve Creek Road. The state grant is for $195,418. That is 48% of the cost of the Creek Road project; 52% will be financed by the borough.  
* May’s article about invasive vines failed to italicize the Latin names of the vine and the weevil.
