Frenchtown Pride Week is June 22-28. Bridge Street will be awash in colorful rainbow Pride flags, installed by the Frenchtown Pride volunteers. All residents and visitors are welcome to join in and enjoy Pride Week, events include a kickball game, sidewalk chalk decoration and a Pride parade and picnic (see [Community Calendar](editions/2026-06/community_calendar) for more details).
