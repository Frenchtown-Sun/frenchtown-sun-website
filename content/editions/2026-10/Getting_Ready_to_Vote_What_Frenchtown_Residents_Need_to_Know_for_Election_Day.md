As residents prepare for the upcoming statewide 
General Election on Tuesday, November 3, they have 
several options on how to submit their vote: 
residents can cast their ballots by mail, take 
advantage of early in-person voting or head to the 
polls on Election Day. The Hunterdon County 
website provides a simple guide to voting at: 
[co.hunterdon.nj.us/970/Election-Voter-Information](https://www.co.hunterdon.nj.us/970/Election-Voter-Information). 

### KEY VOTING DATES & DEADLINES:
* Voter registration deadline: new residents 
registering to vote or those needing to update their 
voter registration details must do so on or before 
Tuesday, October 13. Forms to register for voting in 
New Jersey can be found at the link above. It is 
Voting: What Residents Need to Know Ahead of November 3 Election Day
important to note that these forms must be <u>received
by</u> the October 13 deadline.
* Vote-by-mail: The deadline to apply for a 
mail-in ballot is Tuesday, October 27. Instructions on 
how to request a vote-by-mail ballot can be found on 
the county Board of Elections website at: 
[co.hunterdon.nj.us/2639/Requesting-A-Vote-By-Mail](https://www.co.hunterdon.nj.us/2639/Requesting-A-Vote-By-Mail). Vote-by-mail ballots can be returned 
using the post office (no postage is necessary) and 
must be mailed at least 7 days before Election Day. 
Vote-by-mail ballots can also be dropped off at any 
of the 12 drop boxes located throughout the county 
or returned in person by 3 p.m. on November 2 to 
the Board of Elections office located at 112 Broad St., 
Flemington; a list of the dropbox locations can be found at: 
[co.hunterdon.nj.us/1130/Polling-Places-Ballot-Box-Locations](https://www.co.hunterdon.nj.us/1130/Polling-Places-Ballot-Box-Locations). 
*  The period for early in-person voting is 
Saturday, October 24 – Sunday, November 1. Registered voters can cast a ballot at designated early 
voting centers across Hunterdon County. Locations 
for early voting can be found at the polling places 
and ballot box locations in the link listed above. 
*  In person voting on Election Day: residents 
can vote at Borough Hall (29 Second St., Frenchtown) between the hours of 6 a.m. – 8 p.m.
### WHAT’S ON THE BALLOT:
The list of candidates in the General Election can 
be found on the Hunterdon County website: 
[co.hunterdon.nj.us/2949/2026-General-Ballot-Candidates](https://www.co.hunterdon.nj.us/2949/2026-General-Ballot-Candidates). 

There is a bond referendum on the ballot to 
replace the DVRHS’s fire alarm system; more 
information on the referendum is provided by the 
DVRHS Board of Education: 
[dvrhs.org/page/2026-referendum](https://www.dvrhs.org/page/2026-referendum) (see 
also “Updates from Borough Council” story on page 2). 

For additional voter information, sample ballots, 
or voter registration status, visit: 
[co.hunterdon.nj.us/1409/2026-Voter-Information](https://www.co.hunterdon.nj.us/1409/2026-Voter-Information).
