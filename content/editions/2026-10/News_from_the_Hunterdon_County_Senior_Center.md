The Hunterdon County Senior Center will be 
hosting a variety of programs in October that are 
free and open to the public. For more information 
on programs listed below and for general 
information on the Senior Center, residents can visit 
[co.hunterdon.nj.us/495/Senior-Center](https://www.co.hunterdon.nj.us/495/Senior-Center)
or call <a href="tel:908-788-1359">908-788-1359</a>. 
Membership to the Senior Center is free; you 
must be a resident of Hunterdon County and 60 
years of age. 
Upcoming October programs include:
* A new hybrid strength and balance fitness 
program designed to help older adults stay 
independent and active called VIVO, offered by 
VIVO’s certified trainers. VIVO offers four levels of 
every exercise, from seated to advanced, that can be 
done in person or virtually on Zoom. For more 
information, visit: 
[co.hunterdon.nj.us/DocumentCenter/View/20602/Vivo-Steady-and-Strong-Class](https://www.co.hunterdon.nj.us/DocumentCenter/View/20602/Vivo-Steady-and-Strong-Class).
* Ballroom dancing classes are now available, 
thanks to funding from the Creative Aging Initiative 
Grant through the Cultural and Heritage 
Commission. For more information, visit:
[co.hunterdon.nj.us/DocumentCenter/View/20591/Ballroom-Dance-Classes-1020](https://www.co.hunterdon.nj.us/DocumentCenter/View/20591/Ballroom-Dance-Classes-1020).
* Hunterdon County Clerk Mary Melfi will visit 
the Senior Center to provide information on 
elections, deed frauds, passport cards and ident-a￾shred and answer any questions residents may have. 
For more information, visit: 
[co.hunterdon.nj.us/DocumentCenter/View/20595/Coffee-With-County-Clerk-101](https://www.co.hunterdon.nj.us/DocumentCenter/View/20595/Coffee-With-County-Clerk-101). 
* A new monthly support group for seniors 
who are estranged from their adult children will 
start in October. For more information, visit: 
[co.hunterdon.nj.us/DocumentCenter/View/20600/Senior-Estrangement](https://www.co.hunterdon.nj.us/DocumentCenter/View/20600/Senior-Estrangement).
* Tech Round Table discussions with 
information on various tech topics will resume after 
a summer hiatus. The onsite Tech Help Desk is open 
on Mondays and Thursdays, from 1 – 2:30 p.m. Call 
<a href="tel:908-788-1359">908-788-1359</a> to make your appointment. 
* In addition to the Center’s regularly 
scheduled fitness and social recreational programs 
and classes, there will be a talk and re-enactment of 
Charles Lindbergh’s historic flight as well as a talk 
on Hannah Caldwell from June 1780. To learn more, 
visit the two following websites, respectively: 
[co.hunterdon.nj.us/DocumentCenter/View/20594/Charles-Lindbergh-101526](https://www.co.hunterdon.nj.us/DocumentCenter/View/20594/Charles-Lindbergh-101526) and
[co.hunterdon.nj.us/DocumentCenter/View/20597/Ghost-of-Hannah-Caldwell-101626](https://www.co.hunterdon.nj.us/DocumentCenter/View/20597/Ghost-of-Hannah-Caldwell-101626).
