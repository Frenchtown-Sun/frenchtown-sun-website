**SATURDAY, OCTOBER 10**

* **Rivertown Bridges “Free for All” Community-Wide Free Stuff Giveaway Day, 9 a.m. \- 5 p.m.**
  *Expanding on the success of Buy Nothing "Booty in the Boot" gifting events, Rivertown Bridges encourages residents to set up their "sale" at their individual home, but rather than items being for sale, all items are being given away for free\! This event offers a wonderful opportunity to declutter your home and to pass along items that no longer serve you–or to find some new treasures for free. A great way to keep things out of landfills and an opportunity to connect with your neighbors\! Please visit [www\.rivertownbridges.org](http://www.rivertownbridges.org) to sign-up to be included in the "Free for All".*
