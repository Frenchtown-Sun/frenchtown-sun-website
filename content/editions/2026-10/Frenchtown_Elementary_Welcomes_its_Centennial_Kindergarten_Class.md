As Frenchtown Elementary School (FES) celebrates its historic 1926–2026 centennial year, its halls are once again filled with bright eyes, brand-new backpacks, and boundless potential. 

This September, FES proudly welcomed the class of 2039 — a vibrant kindergarten group that brings 
together twelve enthusiastic young learners. The 
incoming kindergartners reflect the strong roots of 
Frenchtown’s local community, joining from a 
variety of early childhood backgrounds. Most of the 
12 students are transitioning from the Frenchtown 
Elementary Preschool Program, with the remaining 
students joining from other local early learning 
programs, including Secret Garden Montessori, just 
three blocks away from FES. About half of the new
kindergarten class has a parent, grandparent, or 
great-grandparent who attended FES.

In kindergarten, students enjoy many wonderful
stories, play number games, draw pictures, sing 
songs, write stories and create artwork. From 
learning foundational reading and math skills to 
being introduced to foreign languages to building 
lifelong friendships on the playground, these young 
scholars are embarking on an unforgettable journey.

Please join the entire Frenchtown community in 
extending a warm welcome to the new kindergarten 
class and their families!
