On Saturday, September 26, Frenchtown 
Elementary School (FES) will commemorate its 
centennial. Alumni and members of the community 
are invited to attend the festivities, which will 
feature the opening of the time capsule behind the 
building’s cornerstone, remarks by local leaders and 
a presentation on a forthcoming history book by 
local historian Rick Epstein. The celebration is free 
and open to the public. More information can be 
found at [frenchtownschool.org/article/3005260](http://frenchtownschool.org/article/3005260). To support the celebration, 
commemorative merchandise is available for 
purchase online at FES Centennial Gear: 
[spcustomgear.store/frenchtowncentennial/store.html?eid=476196](https://spcustomgear.store/frenchtowncentennial/store.html?eid=476196).

In recognition of this historic milestone, _The Frenchtown Sun_ met with two FES alumni—Wayne 
“Wink” Hummer (Class of 1962) and David “Dave” 
Gano (Class of 1961)—to celebrate their more than 75 
years in the borough and their rich history with the 
school.

At 79 years old, Wink Hummer has lived in 
Frenchtown his entire life. He grew up on 3rd Street 
and married his wife, Ruth, in 1973. After renting a 
home on 2nd Street, the couple bought their current 
home on 4th Street in 1978.

Wink has five brothers, all of whom went 
through the Frenchtown school system. At the time, 
students attended kindergarten, 1st and 2nd grade at 
Borough Hall before moving to the Frenchtown School 
building, which housed 3rd through 8th grade. 

Wink loved his experience at FES and fondly
recalls his favorite teacher, Mr. Puperwinkle, who 
taught 1st and 2nd grade.

Unfortunately, in the 3rd grade, Wink contracted 
scarlet fever, causing him to miss six months of 
class. Following his illness, his mother requested 
that the school hold him back a year to catch up.

Later in middle school, Wink became a three-sport athlete, playing basketball, baseball and tackle 
football. After graduating from Frenchtown in 1962, 
he entered his freshman year at DVRHS. Keeping up 
the tradition, Wink and Ruth’s daughter and son also
graduated from FES and attended DVRHS.

Dave Gano has lived in Frenchtown for 77 years, 
moving to the borough at just one year old.

Like Wink, Dave attended kindergarten through
2nd grade at Borough Hall and graduated from FES
in 1961. He lived in various homes on 2nd and 3rd 
Streets until he was 38. He married his wife, Mel, in 
1978, and they purchased their 8th Street home in 
1981. Dave represents one of three generations of his
family to attend FES—both of his daughters and his
father went through the school system.

Dave played baseball and the tuba throughout 
middle school, frequently hauling the heavy 
instrument back and forth between home and 
school. He fondly remembers competitive recess 
games of kickball and dodgeball behind the school, 
back when the yard had no playground equipment.

Always a Terrier!
