Residents may have seen the Frenchtown Fire 
Department (FFD) supporting members of the 
community throughout the borough, including, most 
recently, during the July storm that caused 
significant tree damage and power outages in the
area. 

The FFD typically responds to 125-150 calls each 
year and there are currently 12 volunteers on the 
FFD roster. 

Firefighting operations in the borough operate 
under a model that is intended to separate 
emergency operations from business or fundraising 
administration. Under this model, the FFD chief 
manages all on-scene emergency operations, 
training protocols, apparatus readiness and 
equipment procurement. The Union Fire Company, 
an independent, non-profit organization, manages 
facilities, organizational bylaws, finances and 
membership. Additionally, Frenchtown participates 
in a county-wide mutual aid agreement that includes 
municipal fire departments throughout Hunterdon 
and Bucks Counties. This agreement ensures a 
reciprocal system where Frenchtown and neighboring departments automatically dispatch extra 
firefighters, specialized equipment and emergency 
personnel to support one another whenever 
required.

The origin of Frenchtown’s dual model dates
back to 1866. The department and the Vigilant Hose 
Company were established with 55 members, a hand 
pump fire apparatus and a hose cart with 500 feet of 
leather hose. After the Vigilant Company's engine 
failed to operate during the 1878 fire that destroyed 
25 homes and businesses downtown, the company 
disbanded. 

In 1888, Borough Council members and citizens 
formed a committee to reestablish fire protection
and on September 12, 1888, the Union Fire Company 
was formed, and the borough swore in fire 
department officers that day.

The firehouse structure located at 31 Second St.
was purchased by the Union Fire Company in 1923 
(the library occupies the original firehouse space).

Today, FFD volunteers must join both the fire 
department as well as the independent Union Fire 
Company. All volunteers must complete more than 
200 hours of training.

The borough provides workers’ compensation
insurance and the borough’s annual budget includes
a line-item appropriation to the independent Union 
Fire Company of $26,000. The budget is spent 
directly on fire new equipment, equipment 
replacements, vehicle repairs and maintenance, 
training, materials and supplies. The Union Fire 
Company hosts fundraisers such as the recent Coin 
Drop; the proceeds of which go directly to the FFD 
budget. It costs $7000 to equip a single firefighter 
with basic gear; the FFD must provide an annual 
accounting of use of borough funds to the Borough 
Council. Additional budgetary line items pay for the 
utilities, fuel and insurances. The ownership of the 
fire trucks, life-saving equipment, and gear is split 
between the Union Fire Company and the Borough 
depending on how the assets were purchased.

Long-time Frenchtown resident, Mike Atheras, 
currently serves as the fire chief of the FFD. Chief 
Atheras is a retired member the New York City Fire 
Department and a former rescue specialist with New 
Jersey Task Force 1 Urban Search and Rescue. He 
was raised in Frenchtown and attended both 
Frenchtown Elementary and Delaware Valley 
Regional High Schools. Chief Atheras joined the FFD 
in 1988 and served as chief from 1996-2000. He took 
on the role again in January 2026. 

Chief Atheras encourages residents to call 911 for 
emergencies rather than the FFD’s direct number, 
<a href="tel:908-996-4423">908-996-4423</a>, which is not monitored 24/7. Calling 
911 ensures an immediate dispatch of the FFD 
alongside mutual aid partners.

Residents who are interested in becoming a 
volunteer member of the FFD can complete the 
application at: 
[frenchtownfd.org/applications](https://frenchtownfd.org/applications). 

Residents who would like to be informed about 
operations of the independent Union Fire Company, 
can use the contact page at the FFD’s website: 
[frenchtownfd.org/](https://frenchtownfd.org/).
