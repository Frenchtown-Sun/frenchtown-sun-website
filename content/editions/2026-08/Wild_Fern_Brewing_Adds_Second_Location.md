---
title: "Wild Fern Brewing Adds Second Location"
edition: "2026-08"
weight: 13
layout: "article"
---
Wild Fern Brewing, located at 62A Trenton Ave.,
announced earlier this summer that the business 
will open a second taproom at 6 Coryell St. in 
Lambertville.

Construction is underway for the new location
along the D&R canal with no official opening date at 
this time.
