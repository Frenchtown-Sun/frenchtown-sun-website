Meet your mayor

BRAD MYHRE was born and raised in Frenchtown Borough and has served as mayor since 2015.

During his tenure, Myhre has helped lead several important infrastructure improvements and redevelopment initiatives that have strengthened the community and supported the borough’s continued growth. In addition to his work in local government, Myhre is a dedicated fan of the New York Giants and Penn State Nittany Lions football, and enjoys spending time with friends, testing his knowledge during trivia nights at Wild Fern Brewery.
