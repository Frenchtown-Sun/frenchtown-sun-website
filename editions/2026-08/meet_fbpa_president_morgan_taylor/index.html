Meet FBPA President Morgan Taylor

MORGAN TAYLOR is the President of the Frenchtown Business & Professional Association, a position that she has held for more than 3 years.

The FBPA, also known as Love Frenchtown, is a volunteer run, member-supported, non-profit organization dedicated to supporting local business and enriching the Frenchtown community. Mobilizing through town events, downtown beautification efforts, strong relationships with other community interest groups and expressing a voice in local government, the FBPA is an active part of civic life in the borough.

Morgan is a native of Hunterdon County and graduated from Delaware Valley High School. In 2011, Morgan’s mom, Dawn Taylor, opened Country Chic, a new and recycled boutique at 47 Bridge St. A few years later it was passed onto Morgan, and she has been the proud owner ever since.

For more information about the FBPA, visit its website at: lovefrenchtown.org/
