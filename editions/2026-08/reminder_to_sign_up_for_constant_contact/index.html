Reminder to sign up for Constant Contact

Residents are encouraged to sign up to receive Constant Contact notices from the borough here.

Constant Contact provides residents with critical information about what’s happening in the borough, including emergency situations and natural disasters. Residents are also encouraged to subscribe to the borough’s YouTube channel where recordings of all Borough Council meetings are posted; residents can subscribe at: youtube.com/@FrenchtownLive.
