TUESDAY, OCTOBER 6

  • Super Seniors, 11 a.m. - 1:30 p.m., Presbyterian Church, 22 Fourth St. Free for adults over 55; yoga with Priscilla, lunch and lecture on new rules for fall clean up by Master Gardeners

WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 7

  • Borough Council Meeting, 7 p.m., Borough Hall, 29 Second St.
  • Clam Night at the American Legion, 5 p.m., 510 Harrison St.

THURSDAY, OCTOBER 8

  • Hunterdon County’s 3rd Annual Veterans Resource Fair, 11 a.m. - 3 p.m., Razberry’s Banquet Hall, 834 State Rt. 12 Free event; featuring local vendors showcasing their products and services tailored to veterans; for more information call 908-788-1102 or email veterans@co.hunterdon.nj.us.
  • Shade Tree Commission Meeting, 4:30 p.m., Borough Hall, 29 Second St.

SATURDAY, OCTOBER 10

  • Rivertown Bridges “Free for All” Community-Wide Free Stuff Giveaway Day, 9 a.m. - 5 p.m. Expanding on the success of Buy Nothing “Booty in the Boot” gifting events, Rivertown Bridges encourages residents to set up their “sale” at their individual home, but rather than items being for sale, all items are being given away for free! This event offers a wonderful opportunity to declutter your home and to pass along items that no longer serve you–or to find some new treasures for free. A great way to keep things out of landfills and an opportunity to connect with your neighbors! Please visit www.rivertownbridges.org to sign-up to be included in the “Free for All”.
  • Lions Club Coin Toss, 11 a.m., Race St. and Bridge St. Rain date: Sunday, October 11, 11 a.m.

TUESDAY, OCTOBER 13

  • Frenchtown Board of Education Meeting, 6:45 p.m., Frenchtown Elementary School, 902 Harrison St.
  • Environmental Commission Meeting, 7 p.m., Borough Hall, 29 Second St.

THURSDAY, OCTOBER 15

  • Community Potluck & Storytelling, 6:30 p.m., Frenchtown Presbyterian Church, 22 Fourth St. All are welcome at the community potluck! If you would like to attend, please RSVP to info@rivertownbridges.org. Please bring a dish to share, a main, side, salad or dessert for 5-6 people. Attendees are encouraged to bring their own beverages, dishware, and utensils.
