Community Calendar

WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 2

  • Borough Council Meeting, 7 p.m., Borough Hall, 29 Second St.

TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 8

  • Environmental Commission Meeting, 7 p.m., Borough Hall, 29 Second St.

SUNDAY, SEPTEMBER 6

  • RiverFest, 11 a.m. – 7 p.m., Bridge St. & Sunbeam Lenape Park
    A street-fair celebration of life on the river featuring two main stages for live music and performances, 50+ art vendors, food trucks and local food vendors, Wild Fern beer tent, Frenchtown Vikings and more! Follow the Riverfest Facebook page for updates or visit lovefrenchtown.org. Bridge Street will be closed for the duration of the event to host local vendors and provide a safe experience for visitors. A bus shuttle will be transporting visitors from Frenchtown Field to the downtown throughout the day. Visitors are encouraged to take advantage of the parking at Frenchtown Field and at Kathee’s General Store.

FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 11

  • Hunterdon County’s 25th Anniversary Commemoration of 9/11 and Memorial Dedication at Hilltop Reserve, 8 a.m., 214 Thatcher’s Hill Rd., Flemington, NJ, The event begins at 8:30 a.m. but please arrive by 8. All are welcome.

SATURDAY, SEPTEMBER 19

  • Hunterdon County Shredding Event, 8:30 – 11:30 a.m., County Complex, 314 Route 12
  • New Jersey Clean Communities 9th Annual Delaware River Cleanup, Start time depends on location, Various locations, For more information register or visit: delawarerivergp.org.

SUNDAY, SEPTEMBER 20

  • Breakfast at Darcy Lodge, 8 – 11 a.m., 39 Everittstown Rd., $12 per person ages 13+, $7 per person ages 6-12, children under 5 eat free; all-you-can-eat buffet.
  • Frenchtown Together, in partnership with Rivertown Bridges Community Group Potluck, 6:30 – 9 p.m., Frenchtown Presbyterian Church, 20 4th St.

TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 22

  • Frenchtown Elementary School Board of Education Meeting, 6:45 p.m., Frenchtown Elementary School, 902 Harrison St.

SATURDAY, SEPTEMBER 26

  • Frenchtown Farmers Market, 10 a.m. – 2 p.m., Sunbeam Lenape Park
  • Frenchtown Elementary School Centennial Celebration, 3 – 6 p.m., Frenchtown Elementary School, 902 Harrison St.
    There will be food, live music and games. Rick Epstein’s newest book Terriers Forever: A Souvenir of Frenchtown School’s Centennial will be available for purchase.
