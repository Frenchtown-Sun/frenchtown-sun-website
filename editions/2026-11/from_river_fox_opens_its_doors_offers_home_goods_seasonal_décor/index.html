From River Fox Opens Its Doors, Offers Home Goods, Seasonal Décor

With a soft opening on Friday, October 9 and opening weekend on Saturday and Sunday, October 10 and 11, From River Fox is a new home goods shop located at 33 Race St.

Owner John, with his background in interior design, and his business partner Joe, who runs Black Shed in Stockton, offer curated and found pieces that are unique and inspired by Frenchtown and the local area.

A sister shop to Black Shed, From River Fox sells fresh flowers, plants, candles, home décor, gifts and seasonal pieces. The store is lushly decorated with leafy plants and vibrant feathers and flowers. John shared that the store’s name was inspired by seeing foxes running across the dunes during a visit to Provincetown, MA and he laughed saying that now he sees foxes everywhere.

From River Fox is available to help with all floral needs, you can contact contact@fromrf.com for your orders.

From River Fox is open Thursdays – Mondays from 11 a.m. – 6 p.m., hours are subject to change.

On Friday, November 20 from 5 – 8 p.m., the store will host a holiday open house replete with small bites, drinks and seasonal cheer.

Visit the store or its Instagram account @fromriverfox to learn more.
