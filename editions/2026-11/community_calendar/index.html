SUNDAY, NOVEMBER 1

  • ArtYard Steamroller Group’s Uncharted Territories Exhibit, 12 - 3 p.m., Apgar House, 12 Bridge St., The exhibit runs from October 24 through November 29; hours are Thursdays through Sundays 12 – 3 p.m.; an opening celebration and poetry reading is set for November 6, 5 - 6 p.m.; Uncharted Territories is a two year long collaborative project uniting 19 poets and 21 artists, culminating in limited editions of 75 hand-bound artist books. Framed prints, broadsides and books will be for sale.
