Reserve Borough Park for personal use

Borough residents may reserve the use of Borough Park, located on Creek Road. The park can be a busy place during the warm weather months, so residents are encouraged to apply early. The application can be found at the Borough website and then emailed to clerk@frenchtownboro.gov or mailed to the borough clerk at Borough Hall.
